Partner With Us
Get Involved

There is a meaningful role for you in this work.

We welcome institutional partners, corporate collaborators, technical experts, volunteers, and individuals who want to support long-term environmental and community outcomes in Ethiopia.

Why Partner With Us

Shared commitment. Real outcomes.

We do not approach partnership as a transaction. Every collaboration we enter is built on shared values, clear mutual understanding, and a genuine commitment to long-term environmental and community impact. We bring 25+ years of field experience, deep community relationships, and tested programme models. We are looking for partners who bring complementary resources, expertise, and a similar horizon.

Partnership Pathways

Ways to work with us

Institutional Partnerships

Development organisations, international NGOs, and bilateral agencies seeking to co-implement restoration, sustainable agriculture, or rural livelihood programmes in Ethiopia.

We bring field presence, community relationships, and programme infrastructure. You bring resources, technical support, and external accountability.

Start a Conversation

Corporate Partnerships

Businesses seeking verified, field-based environmental partnerships in East Africa — including reforestation offsets, supply chain sustainability, and ESG-aligned community investment.

All environmental claims associated with our programmes are grounded in field monitoring and honest reporting.

Explore Corporate Partnership

Environmental Collaboration

Conservation organisations and research institutions interested in collaborative approaches to biodiversity restoration, carbon measurement, or ecosystem science in the Ethiopian highlands.

Discuss a Collaboration

Technical Collaboration

Technology providers, agronomists, hydrologists, GIS specialists, and other technical experts whose capabilities can strengthen specific aspects of our programmes.

We are particularly interested in expertise in remote sensing, precision agriculture, water harvesting, and digital education for rural contexts.

Offer Your Expertise

Individual Support

Individuals who want to contribute to this work — whether through professional expertise, research, communications, fundraising, or other skills.

We consider all applications for individual involvement against specific programme needs. Please describe your background and area of interest when you get in touch.

Get in Touch

Volunteer Opportunities

We work with volunteers in specific circumstances where their expertise directly supports active programme work.

Volunteer placements are assessed case by case. Please contact us with your background and area of interest. We will respond with current availability.

Our Process

What to expect when you reach out

We aim to respond to all partnership enquiries within five working days.

01

Initial Contact

Use our contact form to introduce yourself and describe the kind of collaboration you have in mind. The more context you can share, the better.

02

Introductory Call

We will schedule a call to discuss your interests, share more about our current priorities, and assess whether there is a genuine basis for partnership.

03

Scoping

For serious partnerships, we jointly develop a scoping note or concept paper defining the collaboration framework, roles, expectations, and reporting structure.

04

Agreement & Launch

Partnerships are formalised through a memorandum of understanding or formal contract, followed by a structured programme launch with clear milestones and accountability.
